# Unsub 0.9.0

- **Grouping, Comments and BPM.** Get Info's Info tab now has Grouping, Comments and BPM, laid out as in iTunes 10, and you can change them for several songs at once. Show them as song-list columns with View Options, search and sort by them, and use them in smart playlist rules. Unsub reads and writes them in your files' tags (including the Grouping field the Music app uses), brings them in when you import an iTunes or Music library, and syncs them to iPods.
- **Mini player.** Click the green window button, choose Window › Switch to Mini Player or press Shift-Command-M (Shift-Ctrl-M on Windows and Linux) to shrink Unsub into a compact player with the playback controls, volume and song display. Drag its edge to resize it; narrower sizes drop the display, then the volume. Option-click (Alt-click) the green button to zoom instead. To keep the mini player in front of other windows, turn on "Keep Mini Player on top of all other windows" in Preferences › Advanced.

Songs already in your library show their grouping, comments and BPM after their folder is scanned again: choose **Add Music Folder…** and pick the same folder. Unsub doesn't rescan your music at startup.

Includes 0.8.1's iPod video duplicate check and 0.8.0's Add Videos, playing-indicator and window-size fixes. Physical iPod playback remains unverified. Linux support and Intel High Sierra compatibility remain experimental.

Unsub is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. Bundled FFmpeg, LAME and Opus remain separate, replaceable libraries; their source archives are included with this release.
