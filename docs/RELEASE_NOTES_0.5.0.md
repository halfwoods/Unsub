# Unsub 0.5.0

- Customize the song list with more metadata columns, including Year, Album Artist, Composer, track/disc numbers, dates and audio properties. Show or hide columns, drag to reorder, resize or double-click to fit.
- Save library defaults and playlist-specific layouts. Choose Listening, Library Cleanup or Audio Quality presets, save your own, or apply a layout to other playlists.
- Browse Music through Songs, Albums and Artists. Artist tiles show local album-cover mosaics and counts; open an artist to browse albums chronologically, then songs, with Back, breadcrumbs, Play and Shuffle.
- Use the optional classic Genre | Artist | Album browser to narrow your library alongside search and playlist filters.
- Jump to the playing or paused song with View → Go to Current Song: Command-L on Mac or Ctrl-L on Windows. Playback continues while the app reveals and selects the song.
- Clearer music-import controls and file-drop handling, with more reliable first-run import recovery.
